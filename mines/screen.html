HOW TO PLAY

Open safe cores, then CASH OUT. One unstable core loses the stake. Cash out unlocks after your first safe pick. Your bet is charged once, at START.

Payout = bet × min(50,000, 0.97 ÷ survival probability). The 3% house edge applies once. A zero-pick round starts at 1.00× but cannot cash out. At all safe cells or the 50,000× cap, cashout is automatic. A cap can reduce return below 97%.

Your board stays fixed after reload. This is the existing VAULT client-side demo, not a cryptographically verifiable or server-secret Mines implementation.
