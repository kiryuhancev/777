PAYTABLE & HOW TO PLAY

10 fixed paylines pay left to right, starting on reel 1. All values below are multiples of the TOTAL BET. Only the longest match per line pays; winning lines add together.

3 / 4 / 5 Scatters award 10 / 15 / 20 Free Spins. At most one Scatter per reel; Scatter does not pay cash. Money Fish have no independent payout.

In Free Spins, Collector Wild can land on reels 2–4. Each Collector substitutes regular symbols and collects ALL Money Fish. Multiple Collectors collect the same fish independently. Every four accumulated Collectors award +10 FS. Multipliers: x1 → x2 → x3 → x10; a new tier applies on the NEXT free spin. Marks also count on spins with no Money Fish. Retriggers continue at x10.

Scatter Boost: шанс символа Scatter ×1.28, цена платного спина ×1.5. Выплаты считаются от базовой ставки. В Free Spins и при покупке бонуса доплаты нет. Настройка общая для слотов VAULT.

Free Spins use the triggering bet. Unfinished visual animations are discarded after reload; committed payouts and remaining Free Spins are restored safely. Working title; final logo pending approval.
