======================================================================
3. CURTAIN GEOMETRY
======================================================================

Curtain Feature is a FULL-REEL visual state.

Width:
EXACTLY one reel/column width.

Height:
EXACTLY 100% of the current visible reel viewport height.

The reel may contain 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 symbol cells.
The Curtain Feature still covers the entire physical reel height.

The curtain must never:
- occupy one cell only;
- extend into neighbor reels;
- become thinner than the reel;
- change reel layout dimensions;
- resize the whole grid;
- create vertical page jump.

Implement Curtain as an absolutely positioned visual layer owned by the reel.

The fixed reel viewport remains unchanged.

======================================================================
4. REQUIRED CURTAIN ANIMATION
======================================================================

The visual lifecycle is STRICT:

STATE A — TRIGGER
A SHOW TICKET lands as one normal cell symbol.
All normal reels finish settling first.

STATE B — CURTAIN CLOSE
The feature reel becomes covered by real red velvet curtain halves.
The left and right curtain panels move toward center and fully cover the reel.
Preserve the approved velvet texture.

Use transform / clipping / masking.
Do not animate layout width/height.

STATE C — FIRST OPEN
Curtain halves pull apart like a real theater curtain.
Inside the opening, show:
02_CLOWN_ASSETS/DUO_SCENE_BOMBO_SKINNY.png

The duo scene fills the entire inner stage opening.
It must not appear as two stickers floating over a brown/empty background.

Important:
The actual OPEN_CURTAIN_FRAME must visually remain around the scene.
The user must see the velvet side curtains, top drapery and gold trim.

STATE D — DUEL
The duel/presentation occurs INSIDE the open curtain stage.
The duo artwork remains the visual basis.
Effects may be added:
- spotlight;
- subtle shake;
- glow;
- confetti;
- short emphasis animation.

Do NOT replace the duo image with newly cut/random characters.

STATE E — CURTAIN CLOSE AGAIN
The curtain closes completely again.
This hides the transition to the final state.

STATE F — FINAL OPEN
The curtain opens again.
Now display:
- ONE winning approved clown;
- full circus/stage background;
- the exact approved Wild/multiplier plaque at the bottom.

The final open curtain STAYS OPEN after reveal.

Final result states:
x1 = WILD plaque
x2 = X2 plaque
x3 = X3 plaque
x5 = X5 plaque
x10 = X10 plaque

Do not display "WILD + x3".
The multiplier plaque replaces WILD.

======================================================================
6. BONUS PERSISTENCE
======================================================================

In THE BIG SHOW bonus:

A Curtain that has become an active/persistent Curtain Wild is stored in bonus state.

If that persistent Curtain participates in a win on a later free spin:
- play its active/open presentation as required;
- highlight the character and plaque.

If that persistent Curtain does NOT participate in a win:
- DO NOT replay the entire close/open/duel animation;
- keep it visually present;
- use only a light glow/highlight to indicate its state.

Do not remove active Curtains accidentally between bonus spins.

Persist:
- reel index;
- winning clown identity;
- multiplier/result;
- persistence duration/state according to current bonus math.

Reload must restore it.

======================================================================
