BIG TOP WILDS — CURTAIN FEATURE QUICK IMPLEMENTATION SPEC

SOURCE OF TRUTH CHARACTERS
BOMBO: huge/round/heavy/clumsy physical-comedy clown.
SKINNY: extremely thin/tall/elastic/agile trickster clown.
ONLY these two characters may appear in the Curtain Showdown.

TRIGGER
SHOW TICKET is a one-cell special trigger.
It triggers a full-reel Curtain Feature on its reel.
Ticket itself never expands.

CURTAIN DIMENSIONS
width = exactly triggering reel width.
height = exactly 100% triggering reel viewport height.
independent of 2/3/4/5/6 row count.

ANIMATION
1. Reels settle.
2. Ticket pulses on triggering reel.
3. Red curtain halves enter from left + right edges of reel and CLOSE over full reel.
4. Short hold.
5. Curtain halves OPEN to reel edges, revealing stage.
6. BOMBO + SKINNY perform short predetermined comic showdown INSIDE stage.
7. Loser exits inside bounds.
8. Winner becomes sole character on stage.
9. Final OPEN curtain state remains.
10. Bottom/central result plaque shows only WILD for x1, or only x2/x3/x5/x10 for multiplier states.
11. When all triggered reels resolve, calculate/pay round according to baseline math.

NEVER
- one-cell curtain;
- full-screen curtain;
- curtain wider than one reel;
- generic/random clowns;
- WILD label plus xN overlay;
- RNG in animation callback;
- payout from animation callback.
