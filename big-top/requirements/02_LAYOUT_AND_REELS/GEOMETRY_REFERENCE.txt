BIG TOP WILDS — FIXED VIEWPORT / VARIABLE CELL GEOMETRY

6 reels, fixed outer viewport.
Each reel has constant width = 1/6 viewport width.
Visible rowCount per reel is integer 2..6 and is chosen before spin animation.
cellHeight(reel) = fixedReelViewportHeight / rowCount(reel).

Do not make reel height follow content.
Do not use intrinsic image dimensions to size rows.
Do not change reel rowCount after the visual spin has started.
Do not stretch symbol artwork to fill tall cells; use separate adaptive cell background + aspect-preserving foreground art.

Potential ways = product(reelHeights), but payout ways are determined from matching symbols using the left-to-right evaluator.
