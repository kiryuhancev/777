MAGNET · FIELD MANUAL
5×5 field. The three central rows pay 243 ways from left to right (3 or more matching columns). The top and bottom rows are magnetic buffers; they affect movement and Scatters, but do not pay ways.
Blue + attracts red −; matching signs repel. A neutral ○ creates no ordinary magnetic force, but may be physically displaced by a swap. Only regular pieces move. Each piece moves at most one cell in each phase; occupied destinations swap two movable pieces. Anchors and boundaries cancel blocked moves. Wins stay on the field: no tumble or cascade.
Gravity pulls any nearby regular piece; a charged Magnet Wild pulls opposite signs and substitutes regular paying symbols. Repulsor pushes outward. Polarity Flip changes + ↔ − locally. Chain Magnet can add a phase; maximum 3 phases. Resolution: Gravity → Wild → Repulsor → attraction → repulsion → Flip → Chain. All effects are planned before animation.
